**UK Energy Market Update – 17 November 2025**

Electricity wholesale prices in the UK rose by 3% today, averaging £85 per MWh, driven by tighter gas supply and increased demand. Gas prices climbed 4% to 62 pence per therm amid ongoing North Sea production constraints.  

Ofgem announced a review of the price cap mechanism to better reflect market volatility and encourage investment in low-carbon infrastructure. The regulator also confirmed new rules to improve transparency in green energy tariffs starting next year.  

In renewable energy developments, Ørsted officially inaugurated the 1.2 GW Triton offshore wind farm off the east coast, the largest in the UK, expected to power over 1.5 million homes. Meanwhile, a 500 MW solar park in the Midlands reached full operational capacity, marking a significant expansion in the solar sector.  

Market analysts warn consumers to prepare for moderate price increases through winter due to supply pressures but expect prices to stabilise by early 2026 as renewable output rises and gas imports diversify. Consumers are advised to consider fixed-rate tariffs to mitigate short-term volatility.
