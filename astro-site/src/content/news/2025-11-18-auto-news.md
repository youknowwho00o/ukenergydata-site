**UK Energy News Roundup – 18 November 2025**

**Ofgem Announcements**  
Ofgem has confirmed a freeze on electricity and gas price caps for Q1 2026, citing ongoing volatility in global energy markets and the need to protect consumers during the winter months. The regulator also unveiled new guidelines aimed at accelerating investment in smart grid technologies to enhance network flexibility and support renewable integration.

**Government Energy Statements**  
The Department for Business, Energy & Industrial Strategy (BEIS) reiterated its commitment to reaching net zero emissions by 2050 in a speech today. The Secretary of State highlighted upcoming incentives for domestic heat pump installations and announced a £150 million fund to support green hydrogen production projects across the UK. Additionally, the Prime Minister emphasized energy security in light of geopolitical tensions, underlining increased investments in domestic renewable capacity.

**Industry Market Headlines**  
The UK’s largest offshore wind developer, Seabreeze Energy, announced plans to expand its portfolio by 2 GW with a new Dorset offshore project, expected to come online by 2030. Meanwhile, energy retailer GreenVolt reported a 12% increase in customer subscriptions driven by its new low-carbon tariffs. However, some market analysts expressed concern about supply constraints in battery materials potentially impacting electric vehicle and storage sectors.

**National Grid ESO and BEIS Updates**  
National Grid ESO published its Winter Outlook 2025 report, warning of tight margins due to lower wind generation forecasts and elevated gas prices. The ESO appealed for demand flexibility measures and encouraged more participation in ancillary services markets to maintain system stability. BEIS released data showing a 7% year-on-year rise in renewable energy capacity, predominantly from solar and offshore wind, reinforcing progress towards government targets.
